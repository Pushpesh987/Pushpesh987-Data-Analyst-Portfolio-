### 🤖 3. ChatGPT App Review Analysis
**Tools:** Python · Pandas · TextBlob · Matplotlib · Seaborn · WordCloud · Jupyter Notebook

> Performed end-to-end NLP and sentiment analysis on **193,148 real user reviews** from the ChatGPT mobile app — uncovering what users love, what frustrates them, and what drives star ratings.

**Business Questions Answered:**
- What is the overall sentiment landscape of ChatGPT's user base?
- Which specific words and themes drive positive vs. negative feedback?
- Does NLP-derived sentiment reliably correlate with numeric star ratings?

**Key Findings:**
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| 📋 Reviews Analyzed | 193,148 |
| ⭐ 5-Star Share | 76.5% of all reviews |
| 😊 Positive Sentiment (NLP) | 74.1% — 143,111 reviews |
| 😐 Neutral Sentiment | 22.7% — a key conversion opportunity |
| 😠 Negative Sentiment | 3.2% — concentrated around answer quality & access |
| 🔗 Polarity–Rating Correlation | r = 0.33 (statistically significant) |
| 📝 Avg Review Length | 8.7 words |
| 🔑 Top Negative Signal | `wrong`, `bad`, `cant`, `doesnt` — usability & answer failures |

**Analysis Pipeline:**
1. **Data Cleaning** — Removed duplicates, nulls, non-English reviews; standardized text
2. **Feature Engineering** — Computed `polarity`, `subjectivity`, `review_length`, `sentiment_label` via TextBlob
3. **EDA** — Rating distributions, review length histograms, sentiment breakdowns
4. **Text Analysis** — Top keywords for positive vs. negative reviews; word clouds per sentiment
5. **Correlation Analysis** — Heatmap of ratings, polarity, subjectivity, and review length
6. **BI Dashboard** — 4-panel executive summary synthesizing all findings

### 📈 4. Mutual Fund Portfolio Analysis
**Tools:** Python · Pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · Seaborn · SciPy · Jupyter Notebook

> A comprehensive investment planning tool that simulates, evaluates, and optimizes mutual fund portfolios using historical Nifty-50 stock data (348 trading days × 49 stocks, Jan 2024 – May 2025).

**Business Questions Answered:**
- Which stocks offer the best risk-adjusted returns (Sharpe Ratio)?
- How should a portfolio be constructed for Conservative, Balanced, or Aggressive risk profiles?
- What does the Efficient Frontier look like across 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations?

**Key Findings:**
| Analysis | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 📊 Stocks Analyzed | 49 Nifty-50 stocks over 348 trading days |
| 🎯 Optimization Method | Mean-Variance Optimization via SciPy SLSQP |
| 🎲 Monte Carlo Runs | 10,000 random portfolio combinations |
| 📐 Risk-Free Rate | 7% p.a. (Indian govt bond benchmark) |
| ✅ Key Insight | Diversifying across 5–10 stocks significantly reduces volatility vs. single-stock holding |

**Analysis Pipeline:**
1. **EDA** — Risk-return scatter, Sharpe ratio ranking for all 49 stocks
2. **Portfolio Construction** — Conservative, Balanced, Aggressive allocations from Top 10 by Sharpe
3. **Monte Carlo Simulation** — 10,000 portfolios plotted on the Efficient Frontier
4. **Mean-Variance Optimization** — Mathematical max-Sharpe portfolio via SciPy
5. **Sensitivity Analysis** — How shifting top-2 stock allocations affects Sharpe & volatility

### 👥 7. Employee Attrition Analysis
**Tools:** Microsoft Excel · Pivot Tables · COUNTIFS · Slicers · Dashboard Design

> End-to-end Excel-based analysis of employee turnover across **2,925 employees** to identify the root causes of attrition and recommend data-driven retention strategies. Delivered as a 17-chart narrative dashboard with 5 interactive pivot tables.

**Business Questions Answered:**
- Which job roles, departments, and demographics have the highest attrition?
- What behavioral factors (workload, satisfaction, travel) predict exits?
- Are pay or performance issues driving attrition?

**Key Findings — Ranked by Signal Strength:**
| Rank | Driver | Attrition Rate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Overtime = Yes | **30.6%** (vs 10.5% without) |
| 2 | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Sales Representative role | **40.5%** — 2.5× company average |
| 3 | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Under 25 age band | **39.8%** |
| 4 | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Work-Life Balance = 1 | **30.8%** |
| 5 | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Tenure 0–3 years | **29.1%** |

> **Plot Twist:** Compensation makes **no difference** — Low, Medium, and High income bands all sit at ~16%. We are not losing cheap people. We are not losing weak people. **We are losing good, fairly-paid employees because of how we are working them.**

> **The Root Cause in One Sentence:** The crisis is concentrated in young, entry-level Sales Representatives in their first 3 years — the proximate trigger is unsustainable workload (overtime + travel) that destroys work-life balance. It is NOT a pay or performance problem.

**Projected Impact of Recommendations:** Cutting attrition by 33% through top 3 actions → **Save ~$8M/year**
